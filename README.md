# ESSENTIA – Smart Luxury Jewellery Brand

## Project Overview
Academic business project focused on the creation and development of ESSENTIA, a smart luxury jewellery brand combining elegant design with health-monitoring technology.

The proposed product is an innovative bracelet that integrates discreet sensors to monitor health and wellbeing indicators, combining functionality, exclusivity and premium design.

## Project Objectives
- Develop a new luxury jewellery brand identity.
- Define the product concept and value proposition.
- Identify the target market and customer profile.
- Analyse market size and competitors.
- Develop a marketing and positioning strategy.
- Design a product launch plan.
- Define the business model and pricing strategy.

## Key Areas
- Brand Development and Positioning
- Market Research and Competitor Analysis
- Marketing Strategy
- Target Audience Analysis
- Product Development
- Business Model Design
- Financial Planning

## Key Skills
Brand Strategy, Market Research, Marketing Planning, Business Development, Strategic Thinking and Financial Analysis.
